# New Staff Onboarding Checklist

Welcome to Maple Grove Dental Studio. This checklist covers your first two weeks. Your onboarding buddy, assigned by the office manager, will go through it with you.

## Before your first day

- Sign your employment contract and the confidentiality agreement.
- Send the office manager a copy of your license or certification, if your role requires one.
- Complete the payroll and tax forms sent by email.

## First day

- Tour of the clinic: treatment rooms, sterilization room, X-ray room, emergency exits and the first aid kit.
- Receive your badge, the alarm code and your login for the practice management system and the scheduling app.
- Read the Time Off and Scheduling Policy and the Sterilization and Infection Control Protocol.

## First week

- Complete the online training on patient privacy (HIPAA) and on bloodborne pathogens before working with patients.
- Shadow your onboarding buddy during at least three full days.
- Learn how bookings work: patients book, reschedule and cancel by WhatsApp with our assistant or by phone, and every appointment appears in the clinic calendar.
- Practice the emergency procedure: where the emergency kit and the oxygen are, and who calls 911.

## Second week

- Work your first shifts with supervision.
- Meet with the office manager to review your first week and set your goals for the first 90 days.

## Your first 90 days

The first 90 days are a trial period. PTO starts to be available after this period. At the end of it, you will have a review with the office manager and the clinical lead.

## Who to ask

- Schedules, time off and payroll: the office manager.
- Clinical procedures and infection control: the clinical lead.
- Computers, logins and the scheduling app: the office manager, who contacts our IT provider.
