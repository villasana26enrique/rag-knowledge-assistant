# Time Off and Scheduling Policy

This policy applies to all Maple Grove Dental Studio employees. It is internal and must not be shared with patients.

## Paid time off (PTO)

| Employee type | PTO days per year | Sick days per year |
|---|---|---|
| Full-time (35+ hours per week) | 15 | 5 |
| Part-time (20 to 34 hours per week) | 8 | 3 |
| Temporary or per diem | 0 | 0 |

PTO is earned monthly and can be used after the first 90 days of employment. Up to 5 unused PTO days can be carried over to the next year; any extra days are lost on December 31.

## Requesting time off

- Request PTO in the scheduling app at least two weeks in advance. Requests of five or more consecutive days need four weeks' notice.
- The office manager approves requests in the order they were received.
- No more than one dentist and one hygienist can be off on the same day.
- Time off between December 20 and January 5 is assigned by rotation each year.

## Sick days

If you are sick, notify the office manager by phone or text before 7:00 AM so patients can be rescheduled. A doctor's note is required for absences of three or more consecutive days. Do not come to work with a fever or a contagious illness.

## Shifts

The clinic is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Front desk staff open 15 minutes before the first appointment. Clinical staff work one Saturday per month by rotation; a Saturday shift is compensated with a weekday afternoon off in the same month.

## Shift swaps

You may swap a shift with a colleague who has the same role. Both employees must confirm the swap in the scheduling app at least 48 hours before the shift, and the office manager must approve it.

## Holidays

The clinic is closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. Holidays are paid for full-time employees and do not count as PTO.
