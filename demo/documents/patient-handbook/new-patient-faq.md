# New Patient FAQ

## What happens at the first visit?

The first visit takes about 60 minutes. It includes a complete exam, the X-rays the dentist needs, and a professional cleaning. At the end, the dentist explains what they found and, if you need treatment, gives you a written plan with the estimated cost. The new patient visit starts at $149 without insurance.

## What should I bring?

Bring a photo ID, your dental insurance card if you have one, a list of the medications you take, and recent X-rays from your previous dentist if you have them (taken in the last 12 months). Arrive 10 minutes early to complete the health questionnaire, or fill it in online with the link we send you after booking.

## Do you see children?

Yes. We see children from age 3, and we recommend a first visit around the first birthday to check development. A parent or guardian must come with patients under 18.

## Which languages do you speak?

Our team speaks English and Spanish, and our WhatsApp assistant can also answer in other languages.

## Where are you and how do I get there?

We are at 318 W 57th St, Suite 3, New York, NY 10019, in Midtown Manhattan near Columbus Circle. The closest subway station is 59 St-Columbus Circle (A, B, C, D and 1 trains), a five-minute walk. We do not have our own parking; there are public garages on W 57th St and W 58th St.

## Is the clinic accessible?

Yes. The building has an elevator and step-free access, and our treatment rooms are wheelchair accessible.

## I am nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Many of our patients feel the same way. Tell us when you book: we can schedule a longer first visit, explain each step before we do it, and agree on a hand signal to pause at any time. For some treatments, the dentist can discuss sedation options with you.

## How often should I come for a cleaning?

Most adults should have an exam and cleaning every six months. If you have gum disease, the dentist may recommend a cleaning every three or four months.

## Do you offer Invisalign and implants?

Yes. Both start with a free 30-minute consultation where the dentist checks whether the treatment is right for you and explains the cost and timeline.
