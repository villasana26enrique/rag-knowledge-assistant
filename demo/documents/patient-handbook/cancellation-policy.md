# Cancellation and Missed Appointments Policy

Each appointment reserves a dentist's time just for you. When an appointment is missed without notice, another patient who needed care could not use that time. This policy helps us keep appointments available for everyone.

## How to cancel or reschedule

Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment. You can do it by WhatsApp with our assistant (you will need your booking code, for example MG-7K3QX), or by calling (212) 555-0147 during business hours.

## Late cancellations and missed appointments

- Cancelling with less than 24 hours' notice is a late cancellation.
- Not coming to the appointment without notice is a missed appointment (no-show).
- A fee of $50 applies to late cancellations and missed appointments. For appointments of 90 minutes or longer, such as root canals and whitening, the fee is $100.
- We waive the fee once per year, and always in case of illness or a family emergency. Just let us know.

After three missed appointments within 12 months, we may ask you to book only by phone and to pay a deposit for future appointments.

## Running late

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to shorten the treatment or reschedule it so the next patient is not delayed. Please call us if you are running late.

## Weather and emergencies

If the clinic has to close because of severe weather or another emergency, we will contact you to reschedule and no fee will apply.
