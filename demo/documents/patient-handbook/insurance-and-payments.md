# Insurance and Payments

We want cost to be clear before any treatment starts. Before a procedure, the dentist gives you a written treatment plan with the estimated cost, what your insurance is expected to cover and what you would pay.

## Insurance plans we accept

We are in network with these PPO dental plans:

| Insurance company | Plan type | In network |
|---|---|---|
| Delta Dental | PPO | Yes |
| Cigna | PPO | Yes |
| Aetna | PPO | Yes |
| MetLife | PPO | Yes |
| Guardian | PPO | Yes |

We do not accept HMO or DHMO dental plans, and we do not accept Medicaid. If your plan is not on the list, we can still give you an itemized receipt so you can ask your insurance company for out-of-network reimbursement.

Please bring your insurance card and a photo ID to your first visit. We verify your benefits before the appointment whenever you send us your insurance details at least two business days in advance.

## What insurance usually covers

Most PPO plans cover preventive care (exams, cleanings and routine X-rays) at or near 100%, basic treatment such as fillings at around 80%, and major treatment such as root canals and crowns at around 50%, up to an annual maximum. Teeth whitening is cosmetic and is not covered by insurance. Your exact coverage depends on your plan.

## Patients without insurance: the Maple Grove Membership Plan

Our in-house membership plan costs $29 per month. It includes:

- Two exams and two cleanings per year.
- Routine X-rays.
- 15% off all other treatments, including whitening.

There is no deductible, no annual maximum and no waiting period. You can cancel at any time with 30 days' notice.

## Payment methods

We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, and CareCredit. We do not accept personal checks. Payment for your part of the cost is due at the time of the visit.

## Financing

For treatment plans over $1,000 we offer 0% financing for up to 12 months, subject to approval, through CareCredit. Ask the front desk for the application; approval usually takes a few minutes.
