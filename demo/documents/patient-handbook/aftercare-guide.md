# Aftercare Guide

Maple Grove Dental Studio, 318 W 57th St, Suite 3, New York, NY 10019. Phone: (212) 555-0147.

This guide explains how to take care of your mouth after the most common treatments at our clinic. It does not replace the instructions your dentist gives you at the end of your visit. If something in this guide is different from what your dentist told you, follow your dentist.

## Tooth Extraction

### The first 24 hours

Bite gently but firmly on the gauze pad for 30 to 45 minutes after you leave the clinic. If the bleeding has not slowed down, replace it with a clean, folded gauze pad and bite for another 30 minutes. A little oozing of blood mixed with saliva is normal during the first day.

Do not rinse your mouth, spit forcefully, drink through a straw or smoke during the first 24 hours. These actions can dislodge the blood clot that protects the socket and cause a painful condition called dry socket.

Rest for the remainder of the day and avoid exercise for 48 hours. Keep your head slightly raised with an extra pillow the first night.

### Eating and drinking

Wait until the numbness wears off before eating, usually two to three hours, so you do not bite your cheek or tongue. For the first two days choose soft, cool or lukewarm foods such as yogurt, smoothies eaten with a spoon, scrambled eggs, mashed potatoes or soup that is not hot. Avoid crunchy, spicy and very hot foods, and chew on the opposite side.

Drink plenty of water. Avoid alcohol for at least 48 hours, and longer if your dentist prescribed medication.

### Swelling and discomfort

Some swelling is normal and is usually worst on the second or third day. During the first 24 hours, hold a cold pack on your cheek for 15 minutes on and 15 minutes off. Take only the pain medication your dentist recommended, exactly as directed.

### Cleaning the area

Starting the day after the extraction, rinse gently with warm salt water (half a teaspoon of salt in a glass of warm water) after meals and before bed, for one week. Brush your other teeth normally, but be gentle near the socket.

### When to call us

Call (212) 555-0147 if bleeding is heavy after 24 hours, if pain gets worse after the third day (a possible sign of dry socket), if you have a fever, or if swelling keeps increasing after three days.

## Root Canal Treatment

It is normal for the tooth to feel tender for a few days after a root canal, especially when you bite. This tenderness should improve a little every day.

Until your final crown or filling is placed, the tooth has a temporary filling and is weaker than a healthy tooth. Avoid chewing hard or sticky food on that side, and do not skip the follow-up appointment: a tooth without its final restoration can crack.

Brush and floss normally. If the temporary filling falls out, or if you have severe pain or swelling, call the clinic.

## Teeth Whitening

### The first 48 hours

For 48 hours after a whitening session, avoid coffee, tea, red wine, cola, berries, soy sauce and other dark foods and drinks, and do not smoke. Right after whitening, the teeth are more porous and absorb stains more easily. A simple rule: if it would stain a white shirt, it can stain your teeth.

Drinking through a straw and rinsing with water after meals helps if you cannot avoid dark drinks completely.

### Sensitivity

Mild sensitivity to cold or air is normal for one or two days. Use a toothpaste for sensitive teeth and avoid very cold drinks. If the sensitivity lasts more than three days, call the clinic.

### How long the results last

Results usually last one to three years, depending on diet and habits. A touch-up session can be scheduled when the shade starts to fade. Whitening does not change the color of fillings, crowns or veneers.

## Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings harden completely during the visit, so you can eat as soon as the numbness wears off. Be careful not to bite your lip or cheek while you are still numb.

Some sensitivity to hot and cold for a week or two is normal. If your bite feels high or uneven after the numbness is gone, call us: a quick adjustment fixes it.
